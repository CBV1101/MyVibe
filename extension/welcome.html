MyVibe · Beta guide

Write what you mean, without unwanted hostility.

Private, optional feedback for your own social-media drafts. Your words stay yours.

1. Start writing

Open X, Reddit, Threads or Bluesky and type into a post or reply composer. MyVibe checks the draft locally for explicit attacks, encouragement and a small set of potentially dismissive phrases. Ordinary disagreement stays neutral.

The coach can be closed for the current page session. You can also turn it off globally or for an individual platform in the extension popup.

2. Decide what to change

MyVibe never publishes, sends or automatically replaces a message. If a local suggestion appears, you must explicitly choose it. The score is a provisional language heuristic, not a measure of someone's character or intentions.

Optional AI interpretation requires separate setup and an explicit click on Analyze with AI. That action sends the selected draft and any context you entered to your configured local companion and its AI provider. Do not send sensitive information.

3. Understand your private score

Click Add to my vibe history to save an unverified draft sample. Only scoring evidence, date and platform are saved locally — not the draft text. The score begins at 50 and is a private demonstration, not verified social reputation.

Open My data and score evidence in the popup to inspect or delete saved information. Archive Insights lets you analyze your own imported posts locally; its downloads exclude raw message text unless you explicitly opt in.

4. Beta checklist

  • Try a respectful disagreement and confirm the score stays neutral.
  • Try an explicit personal insult and review the explanation.
  • Disable coaching for one platform and verify it disappears.
  • Test a local rewrite; verify nothing changes without your click.
  • Clear your saved history when finished testing.

Known limits: English-centric heuristics miss nuance, sarcasm and quotations. Site interfaces can change. Public badges, verified account scores and paid subscriptions are not available.

Privacy principle: no automatic AI transmission, no automatic draft saving, no public score claims. MyVibe beta · v0.8
