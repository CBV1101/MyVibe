MyVibes AI settings

AI is OFF by default. When enabled, only clicking Analyze with AI sends your current draft to the local companion, which forwards it to your configured AI provider. Normal typing and local scoring never send text. Do not send private or sensitive content without reviewing it.

The access token is held in extension-local storage for development only. It is not a production authentication method. Configure the Node companion on your own computer using server-side environment variables. Do not enter your OpenAI API key here.
